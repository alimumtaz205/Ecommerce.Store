`editorial-food.png`: original illustrative still life created with the image_gen imagegen tool for this project. Prompt direction: premium editorial food photography of dried apricots, walnuts, almonds and dried fruit in ceramic bowls on a warm ivory/stone surface, natural side light, subtle green and gold Nutragea palette, no text, people, labels, certification marks or documentary claims. The image is visibly captioned illustrative. It is not presented as Nutragea's actual packaged product or as evidence of farmer training. Tool source output: generated_images/exec-c96253c4-fddd-46b8-a6f1-75667f0b77d6.png.
